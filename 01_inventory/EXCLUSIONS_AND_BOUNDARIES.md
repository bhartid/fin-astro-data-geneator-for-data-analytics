# Exclusions and Scope Boundaries

This document preserves the exclusions explicitly listed in the original feature catalogue. These are working boundaries, not permanent prohibitions. The owner requires that valid experimental Western/Vedic combinations not be omitted; any reconsideration must be evidence-based and recorded.

## Western exclusions

## Western features excluded (not valid for this catalogue), with reasons

| No. | Feature | Reason for exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| W-E1 | Astrocartography and local space lines | They map places on the Earth and vary with geography, not along time |
| W-E2 | Interpretive text (aspect, house and Sabian symbol meanings) | Descriptions of meaning, not computed quantities |
| W-E3 | Horary and electional rule sets | Interpretive rules for judging questions or choosing times. The time-based factors they use are already listed above |
| W-E4 | Medical and psychological astrology | They concern the health and psychology of persons and are interpretive, not computed quantities |
| W-E5 | Sunspots, solar flares and geomagnetic indices | Physical measurements of the Sun and Earth, not part of Western astrology and not determined by planetary positions |
| W-E6 | Natal-only static interpretive tables | Fixed tables of meaning with no calculation and no variation along time. (Fixed lookups needed to evaluate included features are included above as fixed reference data) |
| W-E7 | Gann techniques (Square of Nine, price-time squares, planetary price lines, hexagon) | Price-and-time geometry methods built on top of the astrological features listed in this catalogue (planetary longitudes, speeds, aspects, retrogrades, ingresses, declinations and cycles). They consume those features as input and are not astrological features themselves |

## Interpretation of exclusions

- An item excluded from the original time-axis catalogue may still be relevant as an external input, contextual dataset or research extension.
- Gann techniques are not standard astrology features; retain them in a separate research-extension inventory.
- Interpretive prose is not a numerical feature, but a fixed rule or lookup used by a calculation may still be required.
- Geography-dependent mappings are not automatically excluded from the wider project if they can be converted into time-indexed outputs for a specified location; this requires a deliberate scope review.
- Do not silently reverse an exclusion. Record the rationale and obtain owner approval if it materially changes scope.
