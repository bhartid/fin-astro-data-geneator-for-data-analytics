## Family map from the source catalogue

- W1 — Bodies and coordinates.
- W2 — Angles, houses and daily timing.
- W3 — Signs and subdivisions.
- W4 — Dignity and condition.
- W5 — Aspects and geometry.
- W6 — Lunar, eclipse and cycle features.
- W7 — Time-lord and predictive techniques (mostly chart-relative).
- W8 — Comparison of two charts.

## Required approach

1. Preserve all source W-* IDs.
2. Break large families into smaller files when their specifications become substantial.
3. Distinguish Hellenistic, traditional, modern psychological, Uranian/Hamburg and other schools where definitions differ.
4. Record tropical and sidereal options where relevant rather than assuming every Western technique is tropical-only.
5. Keep chart-relative features because company/instrument inception charts may be research inputs.
6. Do not confuse interpretive text with calculable values; retain computational rule tables where they are dependencies.
7. Document exact aspect sets, orbs, applying/separating semantics and event boundaries as explicit variants.
8. Document house system, sect, lots and time-lord formula variants explicitly.
9. Map every feature to common astronomy outputs and event-search capabilities.
