# Financial Astrology and Gann Research Extensions

**Status:** Candidate research domain; not yet inventoried or verified.

## 1. Boundary

This file is reserved for financial-astrology techniques and W. D. Gann-related methods that consume astronomical data, market prices, time, geometry or combinations of them. These are not automatically Western or Vedic astrology features.

## 2. Required catalogue fields

For every method:
- stable method ID;
- exact name and aliases;
- original source (Gann writing, dated publication, documented practitioner source);
- source quotation/page or precise citation;
- whether the method is directly described by Gann or a later interpretation;
- inputs: market prices, dates, planetary positions, cycles, angles, geometry, etc.;
- calculation steps and formula;
- parameter choices and variants;
- expected output and temporal shape;
- relationship to common astronomy features;
- validation/reproducibility approach;
- evidence status and controversy;
- implementation status.

## 3. Research discipline

- Do not attribute a method to Gann without source evidence.
- Separate documented original methods from later adaptations and internet folklore.
- Do not present anecdotal market correlations as established predictive facts.
- Keep price/time geometry, Square of Nine, hexagon, planetary price lines and similar constructs separate from ephemeris calculations.
- Map any required planetary longitude, declination, speed, aspect or cycle inputs to the shared foundation.
- Preserve experimental methods as candidates if the owner wants them considered; label evidence and uncertainty.

## 4. Initial state

No complete Gann feature inventory has been produced in this knowledge base. This domain remains an explicit audit work package.
